Questions organisers ask

Yes. Basic listing and ticketing are free. Paid tiers unlock deeper operational intelligence and communication tools when you’re ready.

No. The guided template is built for organisers with little or no digital experience, most can set up an activity in a couple of minutes.

Who will see my activities?

Essex families looking for children’s activities, we’re focused on this region and audience before expanding.

What happens after someone books?
